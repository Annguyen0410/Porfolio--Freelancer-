05 Process

Three steps, in this order.

  1. 01

    Clarify

    Confirm audience, objective, tone, scope, and format before a word is written.

  2. 02

    Create

    Research, outline, draft, edit, and shape the layout so the copy has somewhere to live.

  3. 03

    Refine

    Apply feedback, proofread, and prepare final files ready to hand off or publish.

Quality & delivery standards

  • Audience fit — Adjust tone, depth, and structure to the reader and the content format.
  • Clear hierarchy — Use purposeful headings, concise sections, and visible next steps.
  • Editorial care — Review grammar, consistency, clarity, and source support where relevant.
  • Ready to hand off — Apply feedback, proofread final copy, keep deliverables organised.

Typical project deliverables

  • Writing

    Article or blog draft, headline options, clear section structure, and a polished final copy.

  • Website copy

    Homepage or service-page text, service descriptions, FAQs, and calls to action.

  • Social content

    Caption options, bilingual short-form copy, audience prompts, and platform-ready formatting.
